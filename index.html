About

I grew up on the move: born in Venezuela and raised mostly in Miami with stints in Bogotá, Guatemala City, and Mexico City before returning to the United States. Always keenly interested in international politics, I majored in International Political Economy at Georgetown University — I thought I’d go into the foreign service after school, but life had other plans for me.

After graduating from Georgetown, I ended up joining the global management consulting firm Oliver Wyman, where I quickly specialized at the intersection of aerospace and defense and private equity. My day-to-day job involved supporting private equity clients with advisory and commercial due diligence for aerospace assets they were buying or selling. Throughout my career, I’ve supported clients on over 45 transactions across the A&D landscape, but with a primary focus on civil & commercial aerospace. I got a lot of reps in, and touched a lot of sub-sectors within aerospace: MRO, PMA manufacturing, production supply chain, distribution, aircraft & engine leasing, FBOs, airport services, defense electronics… you name it, I’ve probably supported at least one transaction in the space.

I left OW in early 2025 — not because it wasn’t going well, it was. In fact, I had risen from an entry-level Consultant to Engagement Manager within three years, the fastest possible ascent to the manager role at the firm. Despite my upward trajectory at the firm, I left because I realized that I didn’t want my “ownership” to stop once the due diligence report was delivered — I wanted true ownership and accountability over the course of a deal lifecycle, and wanted to put my domain expertise to work. Which brings me to what I do now…

What I Do

I still moonlight as a freelance consultant, taking on select commercial and strategic due diligence work for aerospace assets, but that’s not really my focus. My real focus right now is finding and acquiring attractive small aerospace businesses based in North America in the $1–10M EBITDA/SDE range. I leverage my domain and deal expertise to source, diligence, close, and operate great aerospace companies, providing a unique, focused angle in the lower middle market for investors looking to gain exposure to A&D.

Outside the Office

My interest — dare I say obsession — with aerospace and aviation (I’m a self-professed “AvGeek”) is not a product of my day job; it actually started much, much earlier. I was introduced to aviation through flight simulation when I was a kid, and I was instantly hooked. That interest only grew stronger over time, and eventually I obtained my private pilot license. Aside from flying, I also like to cook and enjoy a nice wine (I’m a bit of a snob in this department). I don’t consider myself an athlete, but I do like working out — mainly golf and running. My wife and I live between Mexico City and Miami with Otto, our Bernese Mountain Dog.
